# Limitations (v1.0.0)

*Plain-language note: the honest list of what this dataset does not do, or does imperfectly. Anyone citing NGX-DOL should read it. Numbers here come from `data/processed/qa_report.txt`.*

1. **Derived from PDFs, not an exchange feed.** Values are what NGX printed in its public reports. Where NGX's own reports disagree with each other, NGX-DOL follows the daily price list and records the disagreement in the QA report.
2. **Archive gaps.** About 3% of trading days are missing one of the two daily reports in NGX's archive. Days without an Official List have their untraded universe imputed from neighbouring days (`universe_imputed`). Days without a price list are flagged (`day_trades_missing`), with trading left unknown rather than set to zero. Three calendar gaps longer than 5 days exist (see QA report section 2).
3. **Misdated files.** NGX titled some files with the wrong date. NGX-DOL uses the date printed inside each report. Every title/content disagreement is logged in the parse logs.
4. **Market capitalisation per security starts 16 March 2016.** Before that, NGX's daily bundle did not print it, and v1.0 does not back-fill it. Shares outstanding are *implied* (market cap / close), carried for at most 20 trading days, and will be wrong for a few days around share issues.
5. **ISIN covers currently listed issuers only** (144 symbols, 70% of rows), from a directory snapshot. Delisted securities and symbols that changed (for example ACCESS to ACCESSCORP, GUARANTY to GTCO) are not linked across names in v1.0. Users following a firm through a symbol change must link it themselves.
6. **Corporate actions are not adjusted.** `ret` uses NGX's printed previous close where available. NGX adjusts its reference price for some actions, but bonus issues, splits and rights can still produce large returns. Returns above 11% in absolute value are flagged (`ret_flag`) and excluded from Amihud, but no total-return series is provided.
7. **Board coverage of the Official List.** In some periods the Growth Board and Non-Interest Board are not printed in the equities Official List. Those securities enter through the price list and market-cap list, so their untraded days may be missing. `listed` counts in market totals therefore understate the full listed universe in those periods.
8. **Zero-return and Amihud measures depend on NGX's price-formation rules.** In more than half of stock-months before 2018 the price never moved. Median Amihud shows a roughly tenfold step between 2017 and 2018. The cause is not yet explained; a change in NGX price-formation rules (for example a minimum quantity for a trade to move the price) is a candidate, but it is not confirmed in v1.0. Users comparing liquidity across 2017-2018 should test for a structural break.
9. **Broker tables are top-10 only.** Concentration measures (CR5, CR10, HHI of top 10) are lower bounds on full-market concentration. In {{cr10_value_gt1_weeks}} weeks the printed value shares of the top 10 sum above 100%. NGX's percentage basis is not documented; values are reproduced as printed.
10. **Equities only.** ETFs, bonds, sukuk and funds that appear in the same reports are excluded and listed in `data/interim/excluded_symbols.csv`.
11. **Snapshot data.** The issuer directory reflects 30 October 2026. Re-running later changes ISIN and shares snapshot fields.
12. **Weekly reconciliation.** In weeks where every trading day has a full price list, summed daily volumes equal NGX's weekly activity totals exactly for 97.9% of security-weeks. Remaining differences are listed in the QA report and have not all been explained.
