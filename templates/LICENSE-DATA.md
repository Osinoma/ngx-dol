**Underlying source.** All values are derived from reports published by Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) in its Data Library. NGX is the source and owner of the underlying market data. {{permission_text}} Users must attribute both NGX-DOL and "Source: Nigerian Exchange Limited". NGX does not produce, endorse or verify this dataset.

The raw NGX PDF files are not redistributed. The parser code (MIT license) downloads them directly from NGX.
